AadiVed image licence register

Every stock photo used on aadived.com, with source, photographer, licence and where it is used. Prepared 15 October 2026. 32 images: 17 on the main site and 15 for the Journal (a post's image is only public from its publish date).

About the licence and "licence numbers"

All 32 photos are from Pexels under the Pexels License. This is a standard public licence, so there is no licence number or per-photo certificate. The record for each photo is its Pexels photo ID and page URL, plus the saved screenshot of that page in design-source/licences/ (taken 15 Oct 2026). No Unsplash, Unsplash+, Premium, AI-generated or watermarked images are used.

What the Pexels License allows (read on 15 Oct 2026)

  • Free to use, including commercially. Attribution is not required but is welcome (kept here for the record).
  • Photos may be modified (cropping, colour, WebP conversion).

What it does not allow

  • Identifiable people must not appear in a bad light or in an offensive way.
  • Do not imply endorsement of the business by the people or brands in a photo.
  • Do not sell unaltered copies, redistribute on other stock platforms, or use a photo as part of a trade-mark, logo or business name.

Full text saved: design-source/licences/pexels-license.png and .txt. Unsplash licence copy (reference only, not used): unsplash-license.png and .txt.

If a photo has to be removed or replaced later

  • Find its row here, replace the file in src/assets/images/ with another free Pexels photo (WebP, same crop), and update this register and design-source/stock-credits.md.
  • Run npm run assets:og to rebuild social preview images, then rebuild and push.
  • If Pexels removes a photo, our file stays licensed as downloaded, but note the date and replace it when convenient.

Images not from Pexels

  • Anupriya photos (src/assets/images/anupriya/): supplied by the client.
  • Beach yoga photos (src/assets/images/aadived/beach-yoga-*.webp): supplied by the client. They are not Anupriya or the studio.
  • Instagram crops (ig-yogaday, ig-sanskrit, ig-mono, ig-guided): low-resolution crops from AadiVed's own Instagram, to be replaced with the originals.
  • Logos and icons: AadiVed's own, supplied by the client.
  • The homepage "Studio sessions" card uses a stock photo of rolled mats as a stand-in. Replace it with a real photo of the Megapolis Serenity studio when available.
